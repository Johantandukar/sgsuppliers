Our Story

20 Years of Reliable Supply

Stationery General Suppliers (SG Suppliers) was established in 2005 with a clear purpose: to be the most dependable bulk stationery partner for businesses and educational institutions in Kathmandu Valley.

What began as a small local shop has grown into a trusted B2B supplier serving hundreds of corporate clients, NGOs, schools, and government offices across all three cities of the valley.

We believe a well-stocked workplace is the foundation of productivity. Our mission: ensure your organization never runs out of essentials — delivered reliably at the best bulk prices.
